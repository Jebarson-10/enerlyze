# Carbon estimates

Default electricity factor: 0.675 kg CO2/kWh, CEA CO2 Baseline Database v22,
FY 2025–26 Table S including renewables/captive injection/cross-border adjustments.
Source: https://cea.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/baseline/2026/09/User_Guide__Version_22.0.pdf
Verified 2026-09-28. The same average is used for consumption and illustrative
location-based reductions, not marginal displacement or credit certification.

Bill units are the total baseline; modeled appliance units are a breakdown and are
never added to the bill. Grid factor is editable. Scope is generation-related CO2,
not all greenhouse gases, delivery losses, product lifecycle, or all household fuels.

EPA driving benchmark: 0.393 kg CO2e/mile, converted with 1.609344 km/mile.
EPA urban tree uptake: 60 kg CO2/year, annual average over ten years of growth.
Annual savings are divided by this annual rate. We do not imply immediate uptake
from newly planted seedlings or carbon offsets.
Source: https://www.epa.gov/energy/greenhouse-gas-equivalencies-calculator-calculations-and-references

Catalog product wattage and prices remain samples. No manufacturing/lifecycle
footprint is invented. Wind avoided emissions require site generation data. Solar
savings use self-consumed generation capped by billed annualized load. Equipment,
habit, and solar scenarios are separate, so their savings are not blindly summed.

The startup story is a stylized energy conversion illustration, not a construction
guide or a claim that LPG generation has zero emissions. It plays once per tab
session, can be skipped, and does not play with reduced-motion preference.
