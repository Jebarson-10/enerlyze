The startup story occupies one Three.js world. The camera travels from water filling and LPG heating to a steam turbine, generator, electrical arcs, point-cloud sphere and wordmark. Transitions overlap; models fade as they leave the camera, rather than being swapped at a chapter boundary. Captions update independently. A single continuous progress line replaces section indicators. The scene runs once per tab session and can be skipped or dismissed with Escape. Reduced-motion visitors bypass the intro.

Sound effects are synthesized locally with Web Audio: filtered noise for water, flame and steam; quiet oscillators for machinery and electricity; short sine tones for controls and the final identity. No audio files or audio service are needed. The visitor must enable sound with a tap; sound starts off, can be muted, and pauses when the document is hidden. Assembly ambience only plays while its scene is visible. Intro audio channels clear on completion or skip. This is an illustrative energy-conversion story, not a claim that LPG-generated electricity is emission-free.

Compact layouts keep a single model in a sticky viewing area below the measured navigation height. All four text chapters remain in normal document flow below it. Scroll progress controls separation and reassembly across the entire story; chapter buttons scroll their text below the pinned model. Desktop keeps the existing two-column presentation. ResizeObserver and visibility observers are disconnected, rendering frames are cancelled, and Three.js resources disposed on unmount.
